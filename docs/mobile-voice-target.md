# Georgie mobile voice target

Georgie on iPhone is designed as a low-friction conversational assistant: invoke through App Shortcuts/Siri/Spotlight/Action Button, enter listening immediately, detect the end of a spoken turn quickly, respond aloud, and reopen a short follow-up listening window while the app remains active.

The app does not claim to replace iOS's privileged system wake-word listener. System App Intents are the supported invocation layer; Georgie's own speech recognizer owns the conversation after foreground launch.
