# Georgie Two-Month Readiness Program

## Mission

Georgie must operate as Jason's governed operating-intelligence layer: voice-first and Mac-capable, persistent across interruptions, evidence-first, domain-separated, and able to investigate, prepare, execute within explicit authority, verify outcomes, and recover unfinished work without overstating completion.

This program converts the active two-month workload into an operational contract. A capability is not **ready** merely because code or credentials exist. Readiness requires the capability to be built, connected, authorized, exercised against controlled evidence, and independently verified.

## Non-negotiable invariants

1. Configuration is not health proof; attempted is not completed; missing evidence is not success.
2. Personal, household, and Sierra credentials, storage, policies, approvals, memories, and audit trails remain separated.
3. Every consequential action is exact-scope approval-gated, idempotent, receipted, retryable, independently verified, and recoverable.
4. No readiness transition advances without its canonical physical artifact and required lineage.
5. Superseded, duplicated, ambiguous, or quarantined evidence cannot enter underwriting, lender packages, metrics, or learning.
6. CapitalMatch's inaccurate current metrics remain visible. Georgie must not retrain or claim improvement until lineage certification passes.
7. A one-time preference or exception never becomes a permanent policy without explicit confirmation.
8. Security, credit, borrowing, material funds, contracts, sensitive communications, medical decisions, and material nonrefundable purchases always retain explicit approval gates.

## Capability states

Use these exact states in control reports:

- `designed`: contract exists only.
- `built`: implementation and tests exist.
- `connected`: scoped credentials or device channel are configured.
- `authorized`: applicable policy or approval exists.
- `verified`: controlled end-to-end evidence proves the outcome.
- `degraded`: previously usable but current evidence shows a defect.
- `blocked`: a named prerequisite prevents progress.
- `unknown`: current authoritative evidence is absent.

Only `verified` may be summarized as operationally ready.

## Equipping sequence

### 1. Restore the hands

- Keep all Mac bootstrap/install entry points executable in Git.
- Update and restart the Mac LaunchAgent.
- Verify Accessibility, Screen Recording, and browser JavaScript-from-Apple-Events permissions.
- Run a non-consequential inspect/screenshot workflow, interrupt it, and prove checkpoint resume.

### 2. Certify the business evidence spine

- Reconcile every document across intake, CRM, CM-100, and lender packages.
- Enforce canonical document identity and supersession/quarantine policy.
- Prove the CM-100 physical-artifact readiness gate.
- Repair statement metadata and reject ambiguous or duplicate periods.
- Test new Partner Portal and CapitalApply submissions.
- Certify lender-package contents.
- Rebuild CapitalMatch metrics only from authoritative lender-linked outcomes and classify every miss.

### 3. Complete provider and engineering coverage

- Keep Supabase, GitHub, Vercel, Render, Sierra, Partner Portal, and CapitalApply checks provider-direct where available.
- Add deterministic plan-registry routing, exactly-once dispatch, receipt timeout alerts, bounded delivery retries, and resumable browser workflows.
- Track OpenAI credits, rate limits, queue pressure, deployment failures, RLS warnings, performance warnings, and connector drift as explicit control-map lanes.

### 4. Activate the personal command center safely

- Connect selected personal email, calendar, contacts, tasks, encrypted documents, and read-only financial feeds with separate OAuth grants.
- Capture explicit preferences, budgets, recurring obligations, interruption rules, quiet hours, and emergency/recovery contacts.
- Deliver a daily briefing, unified commitments, bill/cash-flow calendar, subscription review, prepared communications, and weekly financial summary.
- Do not enable spending, payment, credit, security, location, or consequential messaging authority merely because an integration is connected.

## Immediate acceptance checklist

- Mac installer runs directly after a fresh clone.
- `primary-mac` reports the deployed agent version, not only online status.
- A governed browser workflow completes with step receipts, screenshots, persisted assertions, and advisor results.
- The Intelligence and Control Map labels each lane with a capability state and last authoritative verification time.
- Every blocker names the unmet precondition and a bounded next action.
- No business or personal action is claimed complete without outcome evidence.
